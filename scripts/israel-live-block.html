If there is a repeat election: what the gap between the venues implies

Kalshi's prime-minister market resolves "No one" if new elections are called before a government forms. Polymarket's goes to the next person sworn in as prime minister after the 2026 election (deadline 31 December 2027), which would include whoever wins a repeat election. If the two venues price the same beliefs, Polymarket − Kalshi for a candidate is the chance of a repeat election times the chance the candidate wins it, so dividing by Kalshi's "No one" price gives the chance of becoming prime minister after a repeat election. A reading, not a market price: it assumes Polymarket applies its rule this way and ignores the two months' difference in deadlines.
